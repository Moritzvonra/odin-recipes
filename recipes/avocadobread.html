Homemade Cheeseburger

A nice tasty avocadobread for breakfast or brunch. Ideally you add some tomatos and olives to it.

Ingredients

  • 1 Slice of bread
  • 120g Avocado
  • 1 Egg
  • Rucola
  • Beatroot spread

Preperation steps

  1. Cut a slice of bread
  2. Spread the beatroot on it
  3. Fry the egg in a pen
  4. Open the avocado and cut multiple slices and put them on the beatroot bread
  5. Put the egg on top
