Lasagne a la mama

This is the original recipe of the lasagne that my grandmother in Sicily used to make when I was a kid. This will bring you right back to the italian cuisine with the first bite.

Ingredients

  • 500g Lasagne pasta
  • 500g Minced pork meat
  • 200g Mozarella
  • 0.2l Red wine
  • 1-2 Onions
  • 1 Garlic
  • 600ml Tomato sauce

Preperation steps

  1. Cut the onions and garlic
  2. Fry the onions and garlic in hot oil
  3. Add the minced meat and fry until it gets tasty brown
  4. Pour the red wine over and shortly let it stir
  5. Add the tomato souce and let it cook for 5min
  6. Add a layer of lasagne pasta into an oven form
  7. Put a quarter of the meat-tomato sauce on top
  8. Repeat this 2 more times until three layers are created
  9. Top all up with the mozarella
  10. Put the oven form into the oven at 200 degree for 30min
