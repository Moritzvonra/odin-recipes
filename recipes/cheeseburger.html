Homemade Cheeseburger

There is nothing better than a homemade cheeseburger like in this small burger shop in New York. You can alternate the recipe and add bacon, eggs or other topics to make it even more special.

Ingredients

  • 2 Brioche Burger Buns
  • 120g Minced pork meat
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 Spoon of Mustard
  • Breadcrumbs
  • 1 Slice Emmentaler
  • 5 Slices Cucumber
  • 2 Slices Tomato
  • 1 Slice Salat
  • Burger Sauce

Preperation steps

  1. Cut the cucumber and tomato
  2. Mix the meat, egg, mustard and breadcrumbs and form them into a meat paddy
  3. Fry the meat paddy until it gets tasty brown
  4. Add the cheese on top and cover the pan for 1min with a lid
  5. Roast the brioche bun at the same time
  6. Spread the sauce onto the bun and assemble all to a burger
